Contractor Invoice & Estimate (Android) · by DocNod

How to delete all of your data

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → Contractor Invoice & Estimate.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data — or simply uninstall the app.

That's the complete procedure. All documents, customers, signatures, logos and settings are stored only on your device — we run no servers and keep no copy — so clearing the app's data or uninstalling it permanently deletes everything. There is no additional retention period: nothing is kept, because nothing ever left your phone.

What about analytics?

The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (never the contents of your documents). This telemetry is not linked to your identity. If you would like the anonymous analytics identifier associated with your installation deleted as well, email basitafraz8@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and we will process it within 30 days.
