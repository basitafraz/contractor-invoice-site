Privacy Policy

Contractor Invoice & Estimate (Android) · Effective: November 6, 2026

The short version

Your business data never leaves your phone. We don't have servers, accounts, or any way to see your invoices, estimates, customers, signatures, or logo.

Data stored on your device only

Everything you create in the app — business profile, customers, invoices, estimates, purchase orders, delivery notes, credit notes, customer signatures, and your logo — is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Sharing a PDF sends it through the app you choose (WhatsApp, email, etc.) under that app's terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Anonymous analytics

We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) to understand anonymous usage — e.g. how many people create a document or see the upgrade screen — and to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. This telemetry never includes the contents of your documents, customer details, names, amounts, or signatures. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information. The app works fully offline; analytics simply doesn't send when there's no connection.

Purchases

Pro upgrades are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Permissions

The app requests gallery access only when you choose to add a business logo. No camera, location, contacts, or microphone access.

Children

The app is a business tool, not directed at children under 13.

Changes & contact
